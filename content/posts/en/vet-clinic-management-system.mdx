# Smart Vet Clinic Management System

> A modern management system designed for pet hospitals, making clinic operations more efficient and intelligent.

---

## Why Do You Need It?

As a pet clinic operator, have you encountered these challenges:

- Patient information scattered everywhere, difficult to find
- Appointment conflicts, phone bookings prone to errors
- Complicated billing calculations, hard to remember service prices
- Communication difficulties with multilingual clients

The Smart Vet Clinic Management System was created to solve these problems.

---

## Core Features

### 🏠 Dashboard

When you open the system, the first thing you see is a clear dashboard:

![Dashboard](/images/vet-clinic/home.png)

At-a-glance data overview: today's appointments, pending bills, monthly revenue, total patients - all clinic operations at your fingertips. No more switching between different systems, all key information is gathered in one place.

---

### 🐾 Patient Management

**Complete Patient Records**

Every pet has a dedicated profile: basic information, breed, age, weight, chip number, medical history - all at a glance. Supports dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, and many other pet types. The breed library covers common breeds in the market, with support for custom additions.

![Patient List](/images/vet-clinic/huanzhe.png)

Card-based layout for quick browsing of all patients. Supports search and filtering, click to view details. One patient corresponds to one owner, contact information and address automatically linked, no more repetitive entry.

**Smart Patient Addition**

![Add Patient](/images/vet-clinic/tianjiahuanz.png)

Step-by-step form with guided filling. Smart breed selector - type keywords to search breeds, no more scrolling through lists. Can't find it? One-click to add a custom breed, ready for next time.

---

### 📅 Appointment Management

**Flexible Scheduling**

![Appointment Management](/images/vet-clinic/yuyue.png)

Visual time slots, see available times at a glance. Supports custom business hours to adapt to different clinic schedules. Adjustable time slot lengths (15/30/45/60 minutes) for flexible scheduling.

**New Appointment**

![New Appointment](/images/vet-clinic/xinjianyuyue.png)

Check-ups, vaccines, surgery, dental, grooming, emergency... Each type automatically recommends duration, with manual adjustment option. Complex surgery or special cases? Switch to custom mode to set start and end times directly.

**Appointment Reminders**

Supports 1 hour, 24 hours, 48 hours, 1 week advance reminders. Automatic SMS and email sending to reduce no-shows.

---

### 💰 Billing Management

**Service Template Library**

![Billing Management](/images/vet-clinic/zhangdan.png)

Pre-set common services and prices, one-click to add to bill. Filter by category: diagnosis, surgery, medication, examination, grooming, boarding, others.

**Real-time Calculation**

Quantity and unit price adjustable, subtotal and total calculated automatically. Supports discount settings, bills are clear and transparent.

**Flexible Payment Methods**

Cash, bank card, credit card, mobile payment... Supports multiple payment methods to meet different client needs.

---

### 🌍 Internationalization

The system supports four languages:

- 🇺🇸 English
- 🇨🇳 中文
- 🇰🇷 한국어
- 🇯🇵 日本語

Automatically detects browser language, with manual switching option. No barriers for clients from anywhere.

## Design Philosophy

**Simple Yet Powerful**

Apple-style design language, clean and refreshing interface without visual noise. But all features are there, depth hidden beneath simplicity.

**Mobile Friendly**

Responsive design, perfect adaptation for phones, tablets, and computers. Manage your clinic anytime, anywhere.

**Fast Response**

Built on modern cloud-native architecture, pages load lightning fast. No lag, no waiting, smooth and natural operation.

---

## Tech Stack

The system is built with a modern tech stack:

- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript + TailwindCSS
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express
- **Database**: PostgreSQL + Prisma
- **Deployment**: Cloudflare Pages

Lightning fast response, data security guaranteed.
